# Ethical Use Documentation for Conflict Early-Warning System (EWS)

## Purpose
The EWS is designed to flag potential genocide and conflict risks to assist NGOs and international organizations in proactive humanitarian response.

## Ethical Principles
1. **Do No Harm:** The system must not be used to target specific groups or exacerbate existing tensions.
2. **Transparency:** The algorithms and data sources used should be as transparent as possible to allow for independent verification.
3. **Accountability:** Decisions made based on EWS alerts must be subject to human oversight.
4. **Data Privacy:** Personal data must be protected, and signals should be aggregated to prevent identification of individuals.

## Limitations
- **Probabilistic Nature:** The system provides likelihoods, not certainties.
- **Data Biases:** Real-time signals like news and food prices may contain regional or systemic biases.
- **Contextual Nuance:** Automated systems may miss local political or cultural nuances essential for understanding conflict dynamics.

## Responsible Use
Users should cross-reference EWS alerts with ground-truth reports and expert analysis before taking action.
