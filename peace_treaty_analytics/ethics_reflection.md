# Ethical Reflection: Language and Peace

## The Power of Language
Language in peace treaties is never neutral. It can be used to bridge divides or to codify humiliation. The choice between "preventive" and "symbolic" language has real-world consequences for the longevity of peace.

## The Humiliation Trap
Punitive language, as seen in the Treaty of Versailles, can create a "humiliation trap" that feeds revanchist movements. NLP analysis can help identify these patterns before they are finalized in modern negotiations.

## Limits of Analysis
While NLP can classify clauses, it cannot fully capture the historical, cultural, and personal contexts in which these treaties are signed. AI tools should be used to support negotiators, not replace the human understanding of peace.

## Transparency in Scoring
"Effectiveness scores" are based on historical correlations and heuristic models. They should be presented as risk indicators rather than absolute predictions of treaty failure.
