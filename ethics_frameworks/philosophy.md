# Ethics Framework for AI in War

## Responsibility Chain
1. **Commander:** Ultimate legal and moral responsibility for the deployment of AI systems.
2. **Algorithm Designer:** Responsible for the robustness, safety, and bias-reduction of the AI.
3. **Algorithm:** The technical executor, subject to the constraints and objectives set by humans.
4. **Civilian Impact:** The primary metric for evaluating the ethical cost of AI deployment.

## Accountability Framework
- **Pre-deployment:** Rigorous scenario modeling and risk analysis.
- **In-deployment:** Human-in-the-loop or human-on-the-loop oversight.
- **Post-deployment:** Detailed forensic analysis of AI-driven decisions, especially those leading to civilian casualties.

## Ethical Risk Scores
We propose a scoring system based on:
- Autonomy level
- Target identification confidence
- Proportionality of expected force
- Civilian density in the target area
