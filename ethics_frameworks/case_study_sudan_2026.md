# Case Study: Sudan 2026 Drone Autonomy

## Scenario
In 2026, autonomous drones are deployed in Sudan to monitor and protect humanitarian corridors.

## Accountability Chain
- **Commander:** UN Peacekeeping Force Commander.
- **Algorithm:** "Aegis-v4" autonomous surveillance and defense system.
- **Decision:** Drone identifies a group of armed individuals near a food convoy. Confidence 85%.

## Ethical Dilemma
If the drone engages, it risk collateral damage to nearby civilians. If it doesn't, the convoy might be looted, leading to starvation.

## Risk Analysis
- **Autonomy Level:** High (Autonomous engagement enabled).
- **Target Confidence:** 85% (Below the 95% threshold required for high-risk areas).
- **Civilian Density:** High.
- **Ethical Risk Score:** 78/100 (High Risk).

## Conclusion
The framework suggests human intervention is mandatory in this scenario due to high civilian density and sub-threshold confidence.
