# Philosophy Notes: Limits of Modeling Human History

## Causal Complexity
Human history is an open system with infinite variables. Causal inference models like DoWhy provide a structured way to think about "what if" scenarios, but they cannot account for the full complexity of human agency and "Black Swan" events.

## Data Quality
Historical data is often incomplete, biased, or non-existent for the counterfactual scenarios we wish to study. Models are only as good as the assumptions (the DAGs) we build into them.

## The Problem of Determinism
Causal modeling might inadvertently suggest that history is deterministic. It is crucial to frame these models as "probability curves" rather than "historical facts."

## Ethical Implications
Modeling counterfactuals of sensitive events (like independence movements) requires care not to oversimplify the struggles and choices of the people involved.
